test(android): mirror the web suite's ambient, streak and corruption coverage

scripts/compare-test-coverage.mjs diffs what each platform asserts. Four topics
were covered on the web only, and a phone is the worst place to leave any of
them unverified:

- ambient / sound controls. A built-in speaker is the hardest audio case, and
  ambient is the most-reported feature. Now asserts the device has a Web Audio
  context, the 7 beds and the voice/ambient sliders are present, and the engine
  accepts a preset + level (and the originals are restored).
- streak. first day starts at 1, praying twice in one day is idempotent,
  yesterday continues, a missed day restarts at 1. The store keys streak days in
  UTC, so the test seeds UTC dates.
- corrupt localStorage. A truncated entry is a realistic phone failure (an
  interrupted write, or a bad restore). The page is reloaded with a corrupt value
  in place and must recover without a white screen or a NaN counter.
- app-shell vs web, which the previous commit fixed and is now pinned from both
  sides: the browser test asserts Download and Keyboard shortcuts are present,
  the Android suite asserts the impossible download is absent.

compare-test-coverage.mjs is committed as the tool that produced the list, so
the diff can be re-run whenever a test is added rather than rediscovered later.
