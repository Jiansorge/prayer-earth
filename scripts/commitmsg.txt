fix(android-test): give the corruption test room to reload

The test now performs three page reloads (corrupt, verify recovery, verify
restore), and the CI emulator could not hydrate the store fast enough between
them, so a Runtime.evaluate timed out and failed android-smoke. Raised the settle
time after each reload from 3.5s to 7s. It had passed on the physical phone,
where hydration is fast - another case where only the emulator surfaces the
timing.
