test(android): make the no-loss helpers impossible to hang the suite

android-smoke failed with a DevTools timeout immediately after "device
connected", which is where captureNoLossBaseline runs. Whether or not it was the
cause, a test helper must never be able to take the whole run down: it now waits
for the store, and both helpers catch their own errors. A missing baseline
disables the final invariant with a clear message instead of failing the job.
